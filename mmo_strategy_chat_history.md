# MMO Strategy & Fallback Plan: Aion 2 vs. Guild Wars 2 vs. WoW: Forever

## Context & Strategy Overview
* **Current Phase:** Tryhard in **Aion 2** with friends during month one (launch wave, guild setup, initial endgame curve).
* **Target Post-Month 1 Playtime:** ~2 hours / day after work.
* **Evaluation Criteria:** If Aion 2 turns out to be unsustainable, predatory, or chore-heavy, pivot to a fallback MMO.
* **The Contenders for Fallback:** **Guild Wars 2** vs. **World of Warcraft: Forever** (Level 60 permanent horizontal framework).

---

## 1. WoW: Forever — Core Assessment
Sources analyzed:
* Mobalytics Guide: `https://mobalytics.gg/gamebase/guides/wow-forever-explained#15-level-60-cap-11`
* Official Blizzard Portal: `https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/forever`

### Horizontal vs. Vertical Progression
* **Horizontal Framework:** Permanent Level 60 cap prevents expansion-based level inflation where previous content becomes obsolete.
* **Vertical Gear Reality:** Progression within level 60 remains vertical (raid tiers like Barrow Deeps/Hyjal Summit, stat ladders, PvP rank tiers). Classic gear power curves still apply.
* **Parallel Content:** Content updates introduce side zones, parallel leveling paths, side factions, and sidegrade options rather than wiping old content.

### Pay-to-Win & Monetization Concerns
* **Skyborne Race Paywall:** Skyborne elves and their starting zone (Zephras Isle) are locked behind the paid **Skyborne Pack** ($29.99+).
* **Racial Stat Balance:** Skyborne elves include passives/abilities (e.g., 1% haste passive, sprint/mana-regen mechanics, gliding). Min-maxing advantages locked behind real-money character packs create soft P2W pressure.
* **Economy Friction:** High likelihood of gold/token economy influencing consumables, mounts, and GDKP raid culture.

### Respect for Time (The 2-Hour Limit Check)
* **Classic Time Sinks:** Slow travel (no flying mounts initially, manual flight paths), manual group formation, farming for raid consumables, repairs, and profession grinds.
* **The Verdict for 2h/Day:** The rewards have permanence (no level 70 reset), but the daily friction is high. 45–60 minutes can easily be burned on logistics alone.

## 2. Guild Wars 2 vs. WoW: Forever (Head-to-Head for 2 Hours/Day)

| Factor | Guild Wars 2 | WoW: Forever |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Respect for 2h / day** | **Exceptional.** Zero gear treadmill. Jump in, complete content, jump out. | **Challenging.** Logistics, travel, and group assembly eat significant session time. |
| **Progression System** | **100% Horizontal.** Exotic / Ascended gear from years ago remains Best-in-Slot (BiS). | **Hybrid.** Level 60 cap is horizontal, but raid/PvP gear tiers are strictly vertical. |
| **P2W / Cash Shop** | Quality-of-life only (convenience, cosmetics, material storage). No direct combat power. | Paid race/zone packs ($30+ Skyborne Pack), potential gold economy creep. |
| **Endgame Friction** | Extremely low. Instant teleportation, fast LFG, quick entry into Fractals, Strikes, WvW. | High. Extensive travel, manual world navigation, consumable requirements, raid scheduling. |
| **Breaks & Returns** | Return after months or years without falling behind in combat stats. | Returning requires catching up on gear tiers to meet raiding thresholds. |

---

## 3. Decision Matrix & Action Plan
1. **Aion 2 (Month 1):**
   * Push hard at launch with friends.
   * At the 30-day mark, evaluate:
     * Does keeping up require >2 hours/day of daily chores/grinding?
     * Are RNG gear enhancement walls or cash-shop boosts making the experience frustrating?
2. **Fallback Scenario A — Guild Wars 2:**
   * Pick this if you want absolute peace of mind, zero gear invalidation, and maximum return on a strict 2-hour daily window after work.
3. **Fallback Scenario B — WoW: Forever:**
   * Pick this only if your friend group is willing to move together, commit to a coordinated weekend raiding schedule, and enjoys Classic-style world immersion despite heavy logistics.
